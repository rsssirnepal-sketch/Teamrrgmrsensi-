🎯 Drag Headshot Mechanics

On iOS, touch sample frequency differs from Android. Here is how your drag headshot sensitivity curve behaves across ranges:

🚀 Close Range (Shotguns / SMG)

Drag the fire button straight upward in an inverted "J" or quick upward flick. Higher Red Dot values prevent crosshairs sticking to enemy chest.

🎯 Mid Range (AR / DMR)

Requires balanced drag. Don't flick with maximum power; pull smoothly up to collarbone height to lock automatically onto the helmet.

🔭 Scoped Sniping

Keep Sniper Scope between 110–140 to track running targets without overshooting during fast camera re-alignments.

⚙️ Model-Based Preset Philosophy

        

          • Older iPhones (iPhone 5s – iPhone 8): Use tighter sensitivity curves (160–182 General) to avoid touch micro-stuttering on 60Hz displays.


          • ProMotion iPhones (13 Pro – 16 Pro Max): Fast 120Hz refresh rates allow snappier, higher sensitivity (190–200) without crosshairs flying over the target's head.
